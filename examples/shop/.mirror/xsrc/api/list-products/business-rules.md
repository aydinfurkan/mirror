## Context

A customer opens the shop. The page shows all the products that are for sale.

## Goal

- Show each product for sale with its price and its stock.

## Non-goal

- Filter the list or split it in pages. The shop has few products.

## Constraints

- Do not show a product that is not for sale.
- A price change can take up to 60 seconds to show.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show the products for sale, sorted by name.
- Show an empty list when no product is for sale.
