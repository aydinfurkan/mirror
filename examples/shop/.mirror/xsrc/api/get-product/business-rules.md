## Context

A customer opens one product to read about it before they add it to the cart.

## Goal

- Show the product with its description, price and stock.

## Non-goal

- Show the reviews of the product.

## Constraints

- Do not show a product that is not for sale.

## Acceptance criteria

- A customer sees a product that is for sale.
- A customer sees "not found" for a product that does not exist.
- A customer sees "not found" for a product that is not for sale.
