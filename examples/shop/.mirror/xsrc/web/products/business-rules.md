## Context

A customer opens the shop to see what it sells.

## Goal

- Show all the products for sale.

## Non-goal

- Search or filter the products.

## Constraints

- Show the price in euros with two decimals.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show each product for sale.
- Show "Sold out" for a product with no stock.
- Show an error when the products cannot be loaded.
