**Inherits:** none
**Supersedes:** none

## Context

A customer checks what they will buy before they pay.

## Goal

- Let the customer change or remove each item.

## Non-goal

- Check the prices. The shop checks them when it creates the order.

## Constraints

- Show the total from the prices in the cart. The checkout can show a new total.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show each item of the cart.
- Remove an item when its quantity changes to 0.
- Disable "Checkout" when the cart is empty.
