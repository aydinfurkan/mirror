## Layout

- Status bar: the time and the battery.
- App bar: a back button and the product name.
- Image: the product image, the full width of the screen.
- Details: the name, the price and the description.
- Footer: the "Add to cart" button, at the bottom.

## Components

- `Button`: primary for "Add to cart", the full width of the screen.
- `Toast`: "Added to cart." above the button for 3 seconds.

## States

- Loading: a skeleton of the image and the text.
- Not found: the text "Product not found." and a back button.
- Sold out: the "Add to cart" button is disabled and shows "Sold out".

## Responsive

- None.
