## Context

A customer opens the app to see what the shop sells.

## Goal

- Show each product that is for sale, with its price.

## Non-goal

- Search or filter the products.

## Constraints

- Show only the products that the API returns.

## Acceptance criteria

- Show one row per product.
- Open the product when the customer taps its row.
