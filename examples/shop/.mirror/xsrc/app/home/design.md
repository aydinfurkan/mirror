## Layout

- Status bar: the time and the battery.
- App bar: the title "Shop".
- List: one row per product.
- Tab bar: "Shop" and "Cart", at the bottom.

## Components

- `ProductRow`: the product image on the left, the name and the price on the right.
- `TabBar`: two tabs. The open tab uses the primary color. "Cart" shows the item count.

## States

- Loading: three empty row skeletons.
- Error: the text "The shop is not available. Try again later." and a "Try again" button.

## Responsive

- None.
